# SOP: PLR-to-Email Sequence Transformation Engine

> Standard Operating Procedure for breaking down static, encyclopedic PLR reports and blog packs into 10 structured, psychological email autoresponder sequences.

**Related:** If the source is still raw/generic PLR, run `kirby-plr-personalizer` first. Direct-response letter mechanics beyond H-S-L-C live in `kirby-direct-response-copywriting`. Load `audience_profile.yaml`, `voice_dna.yaml`, and `storyline_bank.json`.

---

## 1. The 10 Email Sequence Archetypes

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              Personalized PLR Asset                     │
└────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                             │
                             ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             Sequence Architecture Router                │
├──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Welcome Series (5-part)│ 6. Seasonal / Flash (3-part) │
│ 2. Product Launch (7-part)│ 7. Behind-The-Scenes (4-part)│
│ 3. Educational Nurture    │ 8. Problem-Solution (5-part) │
│ 4. Re-engagement / Winback│ 9. Curated Expert Synthesis  │
│ 5. Case Study Proof Story │ 10. Milestone Celebration    │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Archetype 1: Welcome & Indoctrination Series (5 Emails)
- **Goal:** Set expectations, deliver lead magnet, introduce core philosophy, establish open loops.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Extract foundational principles from PLR; convert into author's personal manifesto.

### Archetype 2: Product Launch Sequence (7 Emails)
- **Goal:** Build anticipation, open the cart, handle objections, inject ethical scarcity, close the doors.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Use PLR "how-to" steps as the problem diagnosis; position the paid product as the implementation shortcut.

### Archetype 3: Educational Nurture Series (5-7 Emails)
- **Goal:** Deliver stand-alone tactical value without burning out the list with constant sales pitches.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Split a 2,000-word PLR report into micro-lessons with actionable 5-minute homework assignments.

### Archetype 4: Dead-List Re-Engagement / Winback (3 Emails)
- **Goal:** Clean unengaged subscribers, trigger replies, re-qualify audience interest.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Pull the most contrarian, shocking claim from the PLR and frame it as: *"Did I lose you on this?"*

### Archetype 5: Customer Success & Case Study Series (4 Emails)
- **Goal:** Build social proof and overcome purchase paralysis.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Wrap the PLR educational framework around a real client or personal transformation story.

### Archetype 6: Seasonal & Holiday Campaign (3-4 Emails)
- **Goal:** Piggyback on calendar events (New Year, Summer lull, Q4 crunch).
- **PLR Adaptation:** Filter the PLR theme through timely seasonal deadlines and urgency.

### Archetype 7: Behind-the-Scenes Journey (4 Emails)
- **Goal:** Deepen loyalty by showing the messy operational reality behind the scenes.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Use PLR to identify best practices, then show how the author struggled to implement them in reality.

### Archetype 8: The Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) Series (5 Emails)
- **Goal:** Systematically dismantle one specific, high-friction bottleneck.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Agitate the root cause of why the PLR's target problem is getting worse.

### Archetype 9: Expert Interview & Synthesis Series (3-5 Emails)
- **Goal:** Borrow authority and present curated takeaways.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Frame PLR sections as debate topics between differing industry viewpoints.

### Archetype 10: Milestone Celebration & Gratitude Series (3 Emails)
- **Goal:** Celebrate a community or business achievement while delivering goodwill gifts.
- **PLR Adaptation:** Repackage high-value PLR checklists/summaries as unannounced free bonuses.

## 2. Email Structure & Direct Response Framework

Every email generated by this skill must follow the **H-S-L-C Formula**:

```
1. Hook (Subject line + Preview text + First 2 lines)
   - Spark curiosity, call out a specific friction point, or state a contrarian observation.
2. Story / Context (The "Human Glue")
   - Lived experience or situational context that bridges into the lesson.
3. Lesson (The PLR Substance)
   - The core actionable takeaway, stripped of fluff.
4. Call to Action (The Next Step)
   - Click a link, reply to the email, or open tomorrow's broadcast (Open Loop).
```

## 3. PLR to Email Sequence Execution

Execute the conversion using the following directives:

**Inputs Required:**
1. **PERSONALIZED PLR ASSET:** {{INSERT_PERSONALIZED_PLR_TEXT}} (if this is still raw PLR, stop and run `kirby-plr-personalizer`)
2. **AUDIENCE:** `audience_profile.yaml`
3. **VOICE:** `voice_dna.yaml`
4. **STORIES:** `storyline_bank.json`
5. **OFFER / CTA:** {{DESIRED_CALL_TO_ACTION}}

**Copywriting Directives:**
1. Generate 3 high-open curiosity/benefit subject lines per email, plus preview text.
2. Convert encyclopedic third-person explanations into punchy, first-person conversational letters.
3. Keep email paragraphs to 1-3 lines maximum for effortless mobile scanning.
4. Include an organic open loop at the end of each email (except the finale) teasing the next day's topic.
5. Provide a clear P.S. section in every email reinforcing the main CTA or sharing a micro-insight.

## 4. Verification Checklist

Before publishing, verify:
- [ ] Source was personalized PLR (not raw); `audience_profile.yaml`, `voice_dna.yaml`, and `storyline_bank.json` were loaded.
- [ ] Are subject lines under 50 characters to prevent mobile truncation?
- [ ] Does every email contain a clear single Call to Action?
- [ ] Has all third-person educational language (*"Individuals should..."*) been converted to direct address (*"When you sit down to..."*)?
- [ ] Is there an organic open loop leading into the next email?
